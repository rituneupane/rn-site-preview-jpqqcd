FAQ

No. I can build in Excel, or in Power BI if you already have it.

Yes. I clean point of sale exports and turn them into reports.

Yes. Files stay private, and I am happy to sign an NDA before you share anything.

Most projects deliver in one to four weeks, depending on scope.
